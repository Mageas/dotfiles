Call the Skill tool twice, for "grilling" and "domain-modeling".

When the interview ends, post a closing summary in the chat, one line per item:

- **Decisions**: what was settled
- **Open questions**: what is still undecided
- **ADRs**: those written, and those proposed but not written

It is the handover to `/to-spec`, short enough to survive a compaction.
